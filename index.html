Mzansi 30 Seconds

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Mzansi 30 Seconds is a pass-and-play party game. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours: your team names, chosen word packs, sound setting and game counts stay on your own device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Team names, rounds, timer length and the word packs you selected
  • Whether sound is on
  • How many games you have played, including today's count (used for the ad rule)

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. None of it is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Advertising

The app is free and funded by ads served by Google AdMob. To show an ad, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier and other device identifiers
  • coarse information such as device type, operating system version and country
  • whether an ad was shown or tapped

This data is used by Google to select and measure ads, and is handled under Google's own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive your personal data from Google - only aggregate earnings reports.

You can limit ad personalisation in Android under Settings > Google > Ads, and in the app itself via Privacy options on the start screen, which reopens Google's consent form where consent is required.

What we do not do

  • No account, email address or phone number is requested.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  • Nothing you type or say during a game leaves the device.

Children

The app is a family party game but is not directed at children under 13, and ad content is capped at a Teen rating. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new date.
